# Store policies text

Formal-Arabic rewrite of 24 Nov 2026 (roadmap Phase G). The meaning of the business rules is the one the owner approved on 19 Nov 2026; the wording is new, and a few standard protective clauses were added (marked in the "What changed" list at the end). **The owner approved this text on 24 Nov 2026** and publishes it from the admin.
The live text lives in the database. It is written and published from **لوحة التحكم ← السياسات**, and this file is only the approved source to copy from.
Every change goes through the owner first, and this file changes in the same commit.

Writing rules used: formal Modern Standard Arabic, the customer is addressed in the plural of respect, only what a customer needs to know (no internal procedures, no technical terms), no rule that the owner has not decided.

## How to publish (once per policy)
1. Open `/admin/policies` and press **اكتب السياسة** next to the policy. If it is already published, press **نسخة جديدة للتعديل**.
2. Copy the four boxes below into **العنوان بالعربي**, **النص بالعربي**, **العنوان بالإنجليزي** and **النص بالإنجليزي**.
3. Check the preview, then press **احفظ المسودة**, then **انشر** and confirm.
4. The footer shows a policy only after it is published, so open the store and check that all six links appear.

- **Wording only (same rules):** shipping, returns, exchanges and warranty keep the same numbers (3 to 5 days, 14 days, 10% deduction, 10 days refund, 10% deposit above 10,000, warranty lengths).
- **Left out because the customer does not need it:** that the warranty lookup shows only the last 3 digits of the phone, and the accounting reason for keeping invoices.
