# Checkpoint: 2.14 Partners v2

Date: 14 Nov 2026

## Done
- Partner record `partners` (Retail): name (unique), phone (Egyptian mobile, optional), join date, default %, basis FinancedParts or WholeShop (one basis per partner), loss sharing, active, notes, row version. Replaces partner agreements.
- FinancedParts: a unit's `partner_id` + optional unit % (else partner default). Set on purchase lines (partnerId), receipt, build part edit, and `PUT /units/{id}/partner` (row version; not for sold units). A percent needs a partner. A whole-shop partner cannot finance units; switching to WholeShop is refused while it has unsold financed units. Units without a partner post no earning.
- Product/category allocations and several partners on one unit removed. Tables `partner_unit_allocations`, `partner_allocation_events`, `partner_agreements` dropped; data migrated into `partners` by `M33_PartnersV2`. `purchase_lines.partner_id` added by `M33_PurchaseLinePartner` (Purchasing).
- WholeShop (D4): monthly share posted by hand after the month ends (Cairo time). Base = store net result after expenses (business report) minus financed partners' order earnings of that month (owner decision 14 Nov 2026); share = % x base; once per partner per month (unique). Loss month: 0 unless loss sharing (then recoverable balance). Active whole-shop percents together <= 100%.
- Capital ledger `partner_capital_entries`: deposits and withdrawals, never edited or deleted; withdrawal cannot exceed balance (409).
- Payouts (was settlements) paid against oldest outstanding earnings.
- Statement data endpoint (capital opening/closing, earned, paid, recovered, lines).
- POS preview returns estimated `PartnerShares` (financed parts; profit split over units by cost) when the seller can see costs. Unit views expose RowVersion and PartnerId (cost-gated).
- Removed: `/partner/agreements`, `/partner/allocations*`, `/partner/settlements`, `/partner/ledger`.
- Rules: `Retail/Domain/PartnerRules.cs`; entities `Retail/Domain/PartnerEntities.cs`; use cases in `Retail/Features/Partners` (PartnerService, PartnerCapitalService, PartnerLedgerService, PartnerMonthCloseService, PartnerStatementService, PartnerEarningsPosting, PartnerFinancing) and `Features/Inventory/UnitPartnerService`.
- Tests: unit `PartnerRulesTests`; integration `PartnerTests` (PartnerPostgreSqlTests), `PartnerMigrationTests`; RetailCoreTests and others updated. Full suite 689/689.
