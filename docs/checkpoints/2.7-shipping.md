# Checkpoint: 2.7 Shipping

Date: 13 Nov 2026

## Done
- Shipping fee and delivery time per governorate group, with a default in shipping settings (27 governorates, ISO codes).
- Upfront = shipping fee + deposit, computed from settings at checkout and stored on the order. `UpfrontDue` shown in OrderView and tracking (0 when paid or waived). POS and pickup orders have no upfront.
- Order keeps a delivery time snapshot (`DeliveryTime`) for delivery orders; shown in OrderView and public tracking. A later settings change never alters an order already sold.
- Status rule: a delivery order cannot move to Preparing until the upfront is collected (409). Rule in `OrderRules`.
- `CheckoutPolicy` carries `ShippingRate(Fee, DeliveryTime)`; Host bridges Storefront shipping settings into Retail (Retail never references Storefront).
- POS: `deliveryFee` optional. When omitted for a delivery sale, fee and delivery time come from shipping settings by the address governorate; a typed fee wins. Preview without a governorate quotes 0.
- Invoice year and number sequence use the business time zone Africa/Cairo (was UTC). `InvoicePolicy` carries the TimeZoneInfo.
- Invoice parts show the part type: `InvoicePartView` gains `categoryId` and `category` (Catalog category name, filled in Host).
- Migration M29 (Retail): `M29_OrderDeliveryTime`.
- Tests: unit `InvoiceRulesTests` (Cairo year), `OrderRulesTests` (upfront), `StoreSettingsRulesTests` (Rates); integration `SalesTests` (delivery time, upfront, POS fee), `InvoiceTests` (part type), `StoreSettingsTests` (shipping rates). Full suite 525/525.
