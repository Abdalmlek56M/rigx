# Checkpoint: 2.15 Money & dashboard

Date: 14 Nov 2026

## Done
- Money accounts `money_accounts` (Retail): name, kind Cash / InstaPay / Wallet / BankTransfer, opening balance + date, active, sort. Seeded: الخزنة (Cash), InstaPay, المحفظة (Wallet), حساب البنك (BankTransfer), opening 0. Default account for a method = first active account of its kind (by sort).
- Movements `money_movements` (append-only): source Sale, Refund, Purchase, Expense, PartnerPayout, CapitalDeposit, CapitalWithdrawal, TransferIn, TransferOut. Balance = opening + movements. No backfill of old payments (opening balance covers history).
- Money moved by: order and POS payments (in, optional account), refund at return inspection (out, optional refund account, default cash), partner payouts (out, method Cash / InstaPay / Wallet / BankTransfer), partner capital deposit (in) / withdrawal (out), purchase seller payment at receipt (out; TradeIn moves nothing, CashAndTradeIn = cash part; balance checked before receiving), expenses (out), transfers between accounts.
- Outflow that would make an account negative is refused (409).
- Expenses v2: editable types `expense_types` (seeded إيجار، كهرباء ومياه، إنترنت، مرتبات، شحن وتوصيل، تغليف، إعلانات، صيانة، أخرى + old categories). Category must be an active type (stored as text). `Direct` needs an order, unit or build link (build link added). Paid from an account or from a partner (exclusive, default cash). Paid from partner = deducted from that partner's dues (payout settlement method `PartnerExpense`, linked to the expense, no account movement, cannot exceed dues).
- Recurring expenses `recurring_expenses`: day 1–28, always overhead. Posted automatically on their day (Cairo time) by the hourly job `RecurringExpenseJob`. A template blocked by low balance stays due and is retried.
- Expense summary (total, by type, previous period + change %, % of sales, fixed monthly) and CSV export (UTF-8 BOM, formula guard).
- Partner capital moves account money (owner decision 14 Nov 2026).
- Dashboard `GET /api/v1/admin/dashboard?from&to` (default last 30 days): KPIs for the period vs the previous period of the same length (gross sales, net profit after expenses and partner share, completed orders, average order, change %); snapshot (stock value at cost, partner dues, cash available + per account); 30-day sales/profit series; to-do counts; breakdowns (lead source, usage, top categories with margin, new vs used margin, full build / split parts / single items); average days to sell used; stock aging > 30 days (count, cost, 20 oldest); partner profits this month. Same sales definitions as the business report (completed orders, business (Cairo) days).
- Dashboard logic in Retail `Features/Dashboard` (`DashboardService`); Host `Retail/DashboardComposer.cs` only adds category names and new requests. Old catalog-count dashboard (`/api/v1/admin/analytics/dashboard`, `AdminAnalyticsService`) removed.
- Migration M34 (`M34_MoneyAccounts`, Retail).
- Rules: `Retail/Domain/MoneyEntities.cs`, `MoneyRules.cs`, `ExpenseRules.cs`; use cases in `Retail/Features/Money` (MoneyLedger, MoneyAccountService), `Retail/Features/Financials` (ExpensePosting, ExpenseTypeService, RecurringExpenseService, ExpenseCsv), `Retail/Features/Partners/PartnerPayouts.cs`, `Retail/Features/Dashboard`; Host `Retail/DashboardComposer.cs`, `Retail/RecurringExpenseJob.cs`.
