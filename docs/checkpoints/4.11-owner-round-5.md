### 2.2 Background effects (dark theme)
The owner asked what we think. **Recommendation: two layers, dark theme only.**
1. *Ambient glow* (CSS only, free): two soft orange/blue radial lights drifting slowly behind the hero and the rails.
2. *Circuit traces* (one `<canvas>`, hero + page header only, not the whole site): thin PCB lines, a faint light pulse travels along some of them, a little brighter near the pointer. 60 fps cap, paused when the tab is hidden or the canvas is off-screen, off for `prefers-reduced-motion`, `save-data`, and low-end devices (`hardwareConcurrency <= 4` → static version), static image fallback.
Skipped: connected-particles network (cliché, heavy), hex rain (noisy, hurts reading), spotlight grid (nice but a second effect competes with the traces), airflow waves (fine later as a section accent). Light theme keeps the calm workshop look.
