## Decisions — delegated to Claude, defaults chosen (part b)
- The orders list has no status counts (no API) and shows the newest 300 orders (API limit); search and channel filter on the page. No export button (no API).
- Recording a payment always uses the store methods (cash, InstaPay, wallet, bank transfer), as the API does; the money account is the server's default for the method (no account picker).
- "Paid" in the payment-status form is for a verified transfer and records no money; the form says so.
- POS always offers pickup (the counter), whatever the online pickup setting says.
- POS "confirm and print invoice": the invoice is issued at Delivered, so the success panel offers "hand over and print the invoice" for a confirmed pickup sale (Preparing → Delivered with `Sales.ApproveOrder`); delivery or pending sales go through the orders screen.
- The board's "new / returning customer" switch is replaced by the phone lookup.
